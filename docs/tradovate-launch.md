# Tradovate launch checklist (REF-112)

0. **Before clearing the allowlist — `TRADOVATE_LIVE_PNL_ENABLED` must be unset or absent in Vercel.** This is the one item that is a compliance requirement rather than an operational one, so it comes before item 1 and has to be checked on the environment rather than assumed from the repo. NinjaTrader confirmed on 29.10 that account-level open P&L falls under our Market Data denial and that redistributing it needs a CME Information License Agreement Reflect does not have, so **Reflect must not show live open P&L to the public.** Unset is off (`src/lib/tradovate/live-pnl.ts` accepts `1` and nothing else — `0` and `false` are both OFF), and off means `/api/tradovate/live` never issues the `/cashBalance/getcashbalancesnapshot` POST and answers `openPnl: null` for every account. The position itself is unaffected: the poll still reads `/position/list`, so the contract, the quantity and the side are reported exactly as before, and the /settings card carries the one sentence that explains the missing number. **Check the variable's absence in the Vercel dashboard, in every environment that serves traders, before doing item 1** — clearing the allowlist with the flag set would publish the figure to everyone who connects. If an ILA ever lands, setting it to `1` restores the behaviour measured on 19.10 with no code change.
1. **Launch — clear `TRADOVATE_OAUTH_ALLOWED_USER_IDS` in Vercel** and redeploy or wait for the next deploy to pick it up. Empty or unset now means every signed-in user may connect (`src/lib/tradovate/allowlist.ts`); leave `TRADOVATE_IMPORT_ENABLED` at `1` or nothing is read or imported for anyone.
2. **Rollback — put one user id back into `TRADOVATE_OAUTH_ALLOWED_USER_IDS`.** One environment change, no deploy and no code revert; all 10 server enforcement points and 6 UI gates follow it immediately. `TRADOVATE_IMPORT_ENABLED` is *not* a substitute: it stops imports and the sync buttons but not new OAuth connects, and `/api/tradovate/today` and `/trades` keep pulling with it off.
3. **Watch call counts.** `calls=` and `heaviestUser=` on the `[tradovate][import-cron]` and `[tradovate][orders-cron]` lines, and the `tradovate_call_counts` rows behind them. §4.3 of the investigation sizes an active trader at 4,500–5,500 calls/day with ~75–80% of it the `/live` poll; a heaviest-user figure far above that is one trader holding the sync button or a tab left open on a position. Those figures were sized with the open-P&L snapshot enabled, so with item 0 off a trader holding a position costs one request per poll **less** than §4.3 predicts — do not read the drop as traffic going missing.
4. **Watch `penalised_until` on `tradovate_connections`.** Non-null means Tradovate has refused us for that trader and every poll and cron now backs off until it passes. A handful is normal; many at once, or one that keeps moving forward, means we are over a rate limit nobody has measured (§4.1 — the documented 5,000/hour figure is unsourced and the vendor FAQ says there is no hard cap).
5. **Watch the orders-cron cursor.** `rotating=` on the `[tradovate][orders-cron]` line and `last_orders_at` on the connection rows: the job is serial under a 60-second `maxDuration`, `/order/list` has `depthDays = 0`, and **a trader the run does not reach loses that day's stop history permanently** — tomorrow cannot pick it up. `rotating=true` with a growing user base is the signal this needs a queue (§4.6 item 1).
